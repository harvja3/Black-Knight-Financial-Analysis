01 / Company Profile

Business Overview

Market position, 2022 performance, and the material corporate event that shaped the fiscal year.

Market Position

Black Knight is a leading software, data and analytics company delivering its services primarily to the U.S. mortgage and real estate market. Black Knight services four pillars: mortgage loan servicing, mortgage loan origination, real estate, and capital markets. They deliver software using private cloud, public cloud, and application programming interfaces (APIs). Black Knight typically has long-term contracts with their software solutions clients containing a base subscription fee with additional fees based on activity.

2022 Performance Highlights

Fiscal year 2022 marked an important period for Black Knight, as they increased their revenue by 5.20%. The reason for the increase in revenue was an increase in their servicing software solutions revenue, an increase in foreclosure related revenues and contract termination fees, increase in origination software solutions revenue, an increase from acquired business, and an increase in license fees.

Although they increased their revenue, their operating income saw a significant drop by 8.18%. This was because their operating expenses increased in 2022 compared to 2021, driven by $37.5 million in higher net personnel expenses from wage inflation above its normal annual increase, $13.7 million in technology costs, $8.4 million in sales and marketing costs (including higher travel costs), and an increase of $8.2 million in client credit loss expense because of a client that is no longer in business. The Intercontinental Exchange (ICE) acquisition also added a decrease in operating income because of $31.8 million in transition and integration expenses.

Material Event — ICE Acquisition Agreement

On May 4, 2022, Black Knight announced they entered into a definitive agreement to be acquired by Intercontinental Exchange, Inc. (ICE). ICE is a premier global servicer of data services and technology solutions offering services to a wide range of customers including corporations, financial institutions, and government entities. The acquisition transaction was valued at approximately $13.1 billion with consideration in the form of a mix of cash (80%) and stock (20%). Black Knight expected the transaction to close in early 2023. The $31.8 million in transition and integration expenses related to the ICE acquisition are a key factor in why Black Knight's operating income decreased in 2022 compared to the prior year.

03 / Strategic Analysis

Financial Performance Analysis

Category-by-category interpretation of Black Knight's ratio results, with strategic implications and risk factors drawn from the 2022 results.

Liquidity

Concern

Black Knight's current ratio is at 1.27 while the industry standard is 1.96, indicating Black Knight is performing below average. This means for every $1 of current liabilities, Black Knight had $1.27 in current assets available to cover that, compared to the industry average of $1.96. Based on the average it appears that the company has limited liquidity available for any short-term obligations and less financial flexibility than other companies.

The quick ratio is also performing below average at 0.76 when the industry standard is 1.76. This means for every $1 of current liabilities, Black Knight had $0.76 in quick assets, compared to the industry average of $1.76, which is much larger. Black Knight didn't have enough quick assets by itself to cover its current liabilities. Because they had lower liquidity than the industry standard, this could make it more difficult for the company to handle short-term expenses and changes in the business. Black Knight needs to focus on its working capital to make sure it can meet short-term obligations.

The lack of financial liquidity can make paying operating expenses and handling unexpected expenses, such as a decrease in revenue, more difficult overall. Several factors increased the pressure on the company's financial position, including the increase in operating expenses related to higher personnel expenses, technology costs, sales and marketing costs, client credit loss, and the additional $31.8 million in transition and integration expenses tied to the ICE acquisition.

Strategic Implications

  • The company has less financial flexibility than other companies in the industry when compared to industry averages.
  • The company can cover current liabilities when considering all current assets; however, the lower quick ratio reflects less short-term liquidity, not enough to cover all current liabilities on its own.
  • The company had lower liquidity compared to industry averages; the company's working capital must be managed better.
  • With less liquidity to cover expenses, if there were a decrease in revenue or other financial issues, this could make it difficult for the company to manage short-term obligations.

Leverage

High Concern

According to Black Knight's debt ratio of 0.55, 55% of its assets are financed with debt. This is above the industry standard of 0.47, indicating the company uses debt financing more than the industry average. This creates financial risk and could limit the amount the company can borrow.

Below the industry standard of 6.50 is Black Knight's Times Interest Earned, with Black Knight's ratio at 2.77. This result indicates Black Knight could only cover its annual interest expenses approximately 2.77 times, making it a higher risk in case of any changes in revenue or the business.

At the end of 2022, Black Knight had $1.66 billion in long-term debt. The company had interest rate risk because the rates on its loans could change. The company previously refinanced its debt through the 2021 Credit Agreement. This creates risk because the interest rate can increase, causing interest expense to increase, which in turn lowers the TIE.

Risk Factors

  • A decrease in revenue could leave the company unable to pay lenders.
  • The company relied more on debt for financing than the industry standard.
  • The company's high debt could make future borrowing more difficult.
  • The company's interest rate directly affects the TIE and how many times over the company can cover its interest expenses.

Asset Efficiency

Concern

Black Knight's receivables turnover ratio is 8.06 compared to the industry standard of 6.80. This means the company is efficiently collecting payment from their clients and is doing so more efficiently than competitors. The higher ratio is a strength for Black Knight, indicating the company is managing money owed by their clients and converting it into cash more efficiently than the industry average.

Although Black Knight was above average in receivables turnover, they were below average for total asset turnover, with a ratio of 0.25 compared to the industry average of 0.80. They are generating less revenue from their assets than other businesses in the industry, resulting in their assets not being used as efficiently to generate sales revenue. Black Knight had a large amount of goodwill and intangible assets. Its low asset turnover is an indication that the revenue generated was relatively low compared to the size of assets it had.

Operational Strengths / Concerns

  • Efficiently collecting payment from clients above industry standard and converting it into cash or revenue.
  • The company's assets aren't being used to generate revenue effectively.
  • The company had large goodwill and intangible assets, which contributed to the overall size of its assets and may have contributed to a lower asset turnover.

Profitability

Concern

Profit Margin. Black Knight's profit margin of 29% is above the industry standard of 19%, meaning the company is making more profit from its revenue than its competitors. This means for every $1 of revenue Black Knight generated, about $0.29 remained as profit after business expenses, compared to competitors that kept an average of $0.19 per $1 of revenue. Black Knight was able to keep a large portion of its revenue as profit, showing they managed expenses well enough to remain profitable compared to industry competition — even with weak liquidity and asset turnover.

ROA. Black Knight's ROA is below average at 7% while the industry average is 12%. This ratio corresponds to the total asset turnover, indicating the company is less efficient at using its assets to generate profit than its competitors. For every $1 of assets, Black Knight generated $0.07 of profit, which is low compared to the industry average of $0.12 of profit per $1 of assets.

ROE. The company's ROE is below average at 19% while the industry average is 25%. This indicates the company is generating a lower return for shareholders than the industry average. For every $1 of shareholders' equity, Black Knight generated about $0.19 of profit, compared to the competitor industry average of $0.25 per $1 of equity.

Profitability Drivers & Outlook

Black Knight excels at generating profit from its revenue, surpassing the industry average of its competitors, which is a major strength for the business overall. A concern is that while Black Knight excels at generating profit from revenue, the company is inefficient and below average compared to the industry at using its assets to generate profit and less efficient in using shareholders' equity to generate additional profits. Overall, the profitability of Black Knight isn't the worst, but it is mixed. The company should focus on generating returns from assets and shareholders' equity, which would make the company more competitive within the software industry.

04 / Executive Assessment

Strategic Conclusions

Overall Financial Assessment: Black Knight was financially profitable in 2022, in particular increasing their revenue by 5.20%. While the company was highly profitable, there are several areas of concern when compared to industry averages. The company struggled with lower liquidity than the industry average, had higher debt than the industry average which could cause financial risk, and had mixed profitability — a high profit margin and receivables turnover, but lower asset turnover, ROA, and ROE. Overall, the company was able to generate profit, but there are multiple areas the company should focus on to improve its financial stability.

Strengths

Black Knight's major strengths were its profit margin and accounts receivable. Its profit margin was 29% compared to the industry average of 19%, meaning Black Knight kept a large part of its revenue as profit. Its receivables turnover was 8.06 compared to the industry standard of 6.80, meaning the company was more efficient than its competitors at collecting money owed by its clients — a major strength that kept the company profitable even amid concerns in liquidity, assets, ROA, and ROE.

Weaknesses

Black Knight's major weaknesses were its liquidity, return on assets, and shareholders' equity returns. Its current ratio and quick ratio were both below the industry average, indicating less liquidity and financial flexibility. Total asset turnover was also below the industry average, indicating the company wasn't efficiently using its assets to generate revenue. ROA and ROE were both below average, indicating lower returns from assets and shareholders' equity than the industry average.

Risks

The major risks are high debt, low liquidity, and variable-rate debt. Low liquidity is a risk because it could reduce the company's ability to cover expenses if there were less revenue or other financial pressure. High debt is a risk because Black Knight relied on debt more than the industry average, and variable-rate debt can change with interest rates, reducing the company's ability to cover expenses and increasing interest expense.

Opportunities

The major opportunities are improving working capital and liquidity, reducing debt, and increasing the efficiency of asset use for generating profit. Focusing on working capital management could help the company handle short-term obligations and improve financial flexibility. Reducing debt would lower financial risk and interest expense. Generating more revenue and profit from assets and shareholders' equity would make the company more competitive in its industry.

Strategic Recommendations

  • Improve LiquidityBlack Knight should improve its working capital management in order to raise its liquidity ratios. The current ratio and quick ratio were both below the industry average. Improving liquidity would help the company manage short-term debt obligations and other obstacles.
  • Reduce DebtBlack Knight should reduce its debt when it can. The debt ratio was larger than the industry average. Reducing debt can lower interest expense.
  • Increase Use of AssetsThe company should focus on generating more revenue from its assets. The total asset turnover was below the industry average. Improving this could increase revenue.
  • Improve ROA and ROEThe company should focus on increasing ROA and ROE. Both were below the industry average. This would increase profit and be better for shareholders.

Investment Perspective

From an investor's perspective, Black Knight was profitable in 2022, and this is the area it excelled at, but it also carried several risks. The company performed well in its profit margin and receivables turnover, indicating strong profits and efficient management of payments from its clients. However, the low liquidity, high debt, and below-average ROA and ROE are risks indicating the company had a harder time with certain aspects compared to its competitors in the industry. The ICE acquisition also created uncertainty, even though it offered the potential for growth. Overall, Black Knight appears to be profitable, but investors would need to watch its debt, liquidity, and assets before investment.
